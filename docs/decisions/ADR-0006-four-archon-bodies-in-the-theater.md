# ADR-0006 — Four archon bodies in the theater

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-10-01 · **Conflict:** C-06

## Context
The High-Level Design and Simulation both say "three or four" archons are physically present, without naming them (High-Level Design §18.1 First generation; Simulation §24.2 Seven theater powers). The Campaign Bible's Movements III and IV need Anodyne, Autophagos, a Heliarch tour and Strategos to be fightable or present, and also use a Pylaios gate, an Aletheia relay and Mneme's crossings (Campaign Bible §5 Campaign architecture, Movements III and IV). The Execution Plan proposes four bodies plus intrusions (Execution Plan §Phase 0 build item 1). See C-06.

## Options
1. **Leave "three or four" unspecified.** P0-04 and P0-06 cannot place or size anything.
2. **Four bodies plus intrusions (recommended).** Anodyne, Heliarch, Autophagos, Strategos are bodies in the theater. Mneme's river, a Pylaios gate endpoint and Aletheia's relays reach the theater without a roaming body.
3. **Seven bodies.** Matches the Campaign Bible's fullest reading but the World Bible says archons are widely separated across a roughly 1,200 km theater, and seven bodies would crowd it (World Bible §States made from services).

## Decision
**PROPOSED:** Adopt option 2. Four archon bodies are physically present: Anodyne, Heliarch, Autophagos, Strategos. The three others are intrusions: Mneme's river crosses the theater, a Pylaios gate has a single endpoint (the World Bible says the gate "never moves", World Bible §Pylaios, the Gate That Arrives), and Aletheia acts through relays. Reasoning: each Movement III arc then has a physical antagonist or site, and awe is concentrated in four bodies the player can learn to dread. The size canon (P0-04) therefore covers the seven archons in the Execution Plan table but needs full body specifications only for the four.

## Consequences
- **P0-02 edits (High-Level Design):** §18.1 (replace "three or four Archons" with the four named bodies and the three intrusions). Key phrase: "three or four Archons". Also §18.0b's closing sentence ("Only three or four Archons are physically present").
- **Not edited by P0-02:** Simulation §24.2 repeats "Three or four bodies"; once Accepted this ADR outranks it.
- **Task specs affected:** P0-04 (size canon), P0-06 (placement of bodies and the Heliarch's tour).
- **What becomes true:** the theater contains exactly four archon bodies at once.
