# P1-14 — March report grammar and renderer

**Agent:** task-implementer · **Size:** M · **Depends on:** P1-13, P1-11, P1-09 · **Branch slug:** march-reports · **Runs on:** either

## Goal
Turn a march journal into a field log written by the people who marched. This is the game's first real writing, and the first feeling gate (G1): Liam reads the three-day log aloud. It must sound like tired professionals keeping a record in a dying world, with the weight carried by concrete numbers and small changed details, never by adjectives.

## Specification

### Policy (`report_policy.ron`)
Per `SimEvent` variant name:
- `symbol`: a grammar root;
- `speaker`: `Leader` | `Character("petra_oss")` | `Reporters` (rotate through the column's reporters by event seq);
- `report`: `Always` | `Never` | `FirstPerDay`.

Starting policy:

| Event | Report | Speaker |
|---|---|---|
| ColumnDeparted | Always | Leader |
| ColumnArrived | Always | Leader |
| SiteReached | Always | Leader |
| StockThreshold | Always | Petra (transport and water) |
| StockExhausted | Always | Petra |
| ColumnDayEnded | Always | Leader |
| MarchWindowStarted | FirstPerDay | Reporters |
| MarchWindowEnded | Never | — |
| ShortHaltBegan / ShortHaltEnded | Never | — |
| DayBegan, ScenarioStarted | Never (they become headings) | — |
| ScenarioEnded | Never | — |

### Bindings (`report/bindings.rs`)
Built from the event, the column state reconstructed from the journal, and the terrain. The `bindings.ron` schema must list exactly these, per root:
- always: `time`, `day`, `column`, `persons`, `speaker_name`;
- when the event has a position: `elevation_m` (from terrain);
- when stock data is available: `water_l`, `water_permille`, `food_rations`;
- for site events: `site`, `total_m`;
- for day summaries: `distance_today_m`, `total_m`;
- for thresholds: `stock` ("water", …), `remaining_permille`.

### Renderer
```rust
pub struct ReportLine { pub seq: u64, pub time: SimTime, pub speaker: String, pub text: String }
pub fn render_report(journal: &Path, scenario: &Scenario, repo_root: &Path) -> anyhow::Result<Vec<ReportLine>>;
pub fn to_markdown(title: &str, lines: &[ReportLine]) -> String;
```
- RNG: `RngCursor::new(seed, StreamKey { domain: Text, subject: event seq }, 0)` per line. One `ExpansionHistory` for the whole report.
- Markdown: `# <column> — march log`, then `## Day N` at each day change, then `**06:40 · Ione Var** — <text>`.

### Content
- Every reported root has **≥ 6 alternatives**. `ColumnDayEnded` and `StockThreshold` have ≥ 10, spread across conditions: early/late day, water high/low, high/low ground.
- Voices: `ione` (terse, dry, protective of the people in her charge, counts things); `petra` (practical, precise about carts and loads, mild complaint as affection). Add stubs for the other four founders for later.
- **Tone rules:**
  - Every line carries at least one concrete measurement or physical detail.
  - No emotion words ("afraid", "hopeful", "grim", "eerie").
  - When conditions worsen, show it through a changed practical detail, without comment.
  - Dry humor is allowed.
  - No archons in Phase 1 lines; the world is quiet, not empty.
- **Calibration examples** (for register only; do not copy them):
  - `"{time:hm}. Moving. {persons} on the road, water at {water_l:L}."`
  - Day summary when water is below half: `"{distance_today_m:km1} today. Petra has moved the water cart to the middle of the column."`
