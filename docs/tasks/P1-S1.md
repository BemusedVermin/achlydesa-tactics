## Specification

### Observer
- At the `inspection_station` site from `red_ledger_sites.ron`, eye height 2 m above `height_cm`.
- Choose the view bearing so the nearest significant ridge lies in the foreground (report the bearing).
- Horizontal FOV 120° over 480 px; vertical FOV 30° over 120 px (square 4 px/deg); horizon row at 96 (the view pitched up so sky dominates).

### Terrain
Per pixel column (azimuth):
- march outward from 30 m to 40 km in 30 m steps (clamped to the terrain bounds);
- elevation angle = atan((h − eye − d²/(2·6,371,000)) / d);
- fill pixels below the running maximum angle;
- shade by distance (farther ⇒ lighter, atmospheric) and by the local gradient facing a sun at azimuth 250°, elevation 12°.

### Heliarch (raymarched SDF)
Placed at 5 km horizontal distance on the view bearing's center, altitude = size canon `cruise` (3,000 m). Body frame:
- body: ellipsoid, semi-axes (1200, 450, 250) m, **belly up**;
- dorsal fin pointing down;
- flukes: flattened ellipsoid at the tail;
- two pectoral fins;
- a broken halo: a torus arc of ~250° around the head;
- 3–5 oil curtains: thin vertical slabs from the underside reaching down 1,500 m, with density fading toward the bottom.

Surface shading: mirror plates as a domain-repeated cell pattern that reflects sky luminance. The underside faces the sun and is brightest, per the World Bible.

### Palette
Quantize with a 4 × 4 Bayer ordered dither to four tokens: Ink `#352F3F`, Secondary ink `#655B68`, Paper `#E9DFC9`, and Anomalous `#884653`, reserved for the halo and the oil.
