# H-3 — Choose the terrain window

**Agent:** Liam · **Size:** S · **Depends on:** P0-05 · **Branch slug:** — · **Runs on:** human

## Goal
Pick the ground the Red Ledger caravan will cross.

## Steps
1. Look at `docs/canon/terrain_candidates/*.png` and read `docs/canon/TERRAIN_CANDIDATES.md`.
2. In `docs/decisions/ADR-0100-terrain-source.md`, mark your choice in the Decision section and set `Status: Accepted`. Commit through a PR.
3. Download the source tiles for the chosen window into `data/raw/terrain/`, using the commands in the ADR. They stay local (gitignored). Tasks marked **Runs on: local** need them.

## Done when
The ADR is Accepted and merged, and the tiles are on disk. Close this issue.
