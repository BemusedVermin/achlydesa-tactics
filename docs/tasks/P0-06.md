# P0-06 — Red Ledger region placement

**Agent:** canon-editor · **Size:** M · **Depends on:** H-3, H-2 · **Branch slug:** red-ledger-region · **Runs on:** local

## Goal
Lay the Red Ledger campaign (Movements I–II) onto the chosen terrain window: sites, roads, the Heliarch's sky-tour path, the 96 × 24 km strip used in Phase 1, and the 30 × 30 km tactical corridor for Phase 3. From here on the game has a real geography.

## Specification

### Local frame
x = east, y = north, integer meters, origin at the SW corner of the region **after** the window transform. Game north may differ from real north.

### `window.ron`
```ron
(
  source: "copernicus-glo30",            // per ADR-0100
  tiles: ["…"],                          // exact tile filenames under data/raw/terrain/
  sw_lat_lon: (29.0, 35.0),              // real-world SW corner of the untransformed window
  extent_m: (150000, 150000),
  transform: (rotate_deg: 0, mirror_x: false),  // rotate ∈ {0, 90, 180, 270}
  base_spacing_m: 30,
  strip_p1: (min: (x_m, y_m), max: (x_m, y_m)),       // exactly 96000 × 24000 m, axis-aligned
  corridor_p3: (min: (x_m, y_m), max: (x_m, y_m)),    // exactly 30000 × 30000 m, inside strip_p1 where possible
)
```

### `red_ledger_sites.ron`
Entries: `(id, name, kind, pos: (x_m, y_m), canon: "file §section" | "PROPOSED", notes)`. Required ids:
- `cistern_camp`
- `inspection_station` (Captain Varo Kest's checkpoint, on the Dry Meridian road)
- `roadside_terminal`
- `reedbank` (on a secondary river crossing)
- `excluded_settlement` (on Sera's less exposed approach)
- `reserve_depot` (Olan's)
- `marches_garrison` (Damar)
- `three_crossings_a`, `three_crossings_b`, `three_crossings_c`
- `lamp_ward`

Invent names only where canon has none; mark them PROPOSED and give them the Greek-inflected or plain-functional register the World Bible uses for places.

### `red_ledger_routes.ron`
Polylines as lists of `(x_m, y_m)` vertices every ≤500 m, each with `(id, name, kind: road | track | approach, condition: intact | worn | broken)`. Required:
- `dry_meridian` (Cistern Camp → inspection station → roadside terminal → toward Lamp Ward);
- `reedbank_spur`;
- `sera_approach` (through the excluded settlement);
- at least one road reaching each of the three crossings.

Place roads where the terrain allows; they must avoid slopes over 150‰ except at named passes.

### `heliarch_tour` (in `red_ledger_routes.ron`)
A path with `altitude_m` per vertex and a note on the announced timing (Campaign Bible §5 Movement III mentions the tour). It must pass within sight of the Dry Meridian road during Movement I, to allow the first-sighting beat.

### Distance constraints (verify with a script; report the numbers)
- Road length Cistern Camp → inspection station: 20–45 km (one to two days on foot, per High-Level Design §4.1b).
- Inspection station → Reedbank: 20–45 km.
- Road length Cistern Camp → Reedbank lies inside `strip_p1` and is 70–90 km. The Phase 1 three-day march runs this.
- `corridor_p3` contains the inspection station, a ridge within 5 km of it, and a crossing.
- Three crossings: each pair of crossings is 5–20 km apart along the watercourse.

### Overlay (`region_overlay.py` → `red_ledger_region.png`)
Hillshade of the transformed window with sites, routes, the Heliarch path, and both boxes (`strip_p1`, `corridor_p3`), all labeled. 1500 × 1500 px. Use the atlas day palette, with friendly teal `#236965` for sites and anomalous `#884653` for the Heliarch path.

### `RED_LEDGER_REGION.md`
The image. A table of sites with coordinates and citations. Route lengths and the constraint checks. One paragraph per Movement I–II describing how the story moves over this ground: distances, heights, sightlines.

- All required site ids and routes present; RON parses (the script loads it).
- Every site has a canon citation or is marked PROPOSED.
