# P0-03 — Experience Bible

**Agent:** canon-editor · **Size:** L · **Depends on:** H-2 · **Branch slug:** experience-bible · **Runs on:** either

## Goal
Turn Execution Plan §2 into a buildable specification: the systems that make players feel **awe** at colossal archons, **despair** at irrecoverable loss, and **hope** that is material and earned, plus the satire channel through which the archons speak. Phase 2+ engineers will implement directly from this document, so every mechanism needs inputs, rules, and a testable invariant.

## Specification
The document starts with `Status: PROPOSED` and has these sections, in this order.

1. **Purpose and three feelings.** One paragraph each for awe, despair, hope, and satire. State what each feeling is *not* (awe is not spectacle for its own sake; despair is not punishment; hope is not a score).
2. **Inviolable rules.** Numbered, each phrased as a testable invariant. Must include:
   - affect outputs are a pure function of the HQ projection;
   - affect never changes command information, controls, or measurements;
   - every emotional beat is caused by a simulated event (no scripted feelings);
   - the camera never moves without consent.
   For each, name the test that will verify it (e.g. "hidden-state equivalence test extended to affect outputs, Field Atlas §23.2").
3. **The affect bus.** For each signal (`awe`, `dread`, `grief`, `hope`, `tension`):
   - range and units: integer 0–1000;
   - inputs: exact projection fields, named even if they do not exist yet, and flagged "requires Phase N";
   - a **proposed formula** in integer arithmetic, with decay constants;
   - update cadence;
   - which consumers read it.
   Include a consumer table: music layers, ambience, palette temperature (allowed range: ±3% hue on non-command layers only), report register, plate lighting.
4. **Awe.**
   - (a) Angular size as the metric: θ = 2·atan(s / 2d), referencing `docs/canon/ARCHON_SIZE_CANON.md` if present (write "see P0-04" if not).
   - (b) Atlas-breaking catalogue: each effect with its trigger, the layer it touches, why it is honest, and what it must never touch.
   - (c) Horizon plate: plates are reports; resolution, FOV, palette, dithering; when they are generated; how they travel; staleness display.
   - (d) First-sighting protocol as additions to the Field Atlas §19 decision-request state machine: states, transitions, what makes it bypass reduced notifications.
   - (e) Sound signatures for Heliarch, Anodyne, Autophagos, and Strategos as synthesis recipes: oscillators, noise, envelopes, and which signal drives which parameter.
   - (f) **The flinch** (World Bible §The flinch): the top of the awe ladder. Specify how a flinch reaches the player through each channel: atlas, plates, sound, reports, proclamations, and the Last-Of and Kept systems when it costs someone. Give its affect-bus signature. It must follow the World Bible's evidence rule: trust, then a plain break, then a distant cost, never explained. Propose placements per Movement, consistent with Campaign Bible §10's open design question, and mark them PROPOSED.
5. **Despair.** Specify the Roll card, ink fade of the missing, graves on the atlas, the Last-Of registry, the caravan theme's voices, and the despair floor. For each give triggers, data needed, UI surface (Field Atlas §8 inventory terms), and what the player can and cannot do with it. The Roll must: list each confirmed death; show one generated line from the survivor with the highest affinity; state what the action protected, or state plainly that nothing was protected; be impossible to batch-dismiss.
6. **Hope.**
   - The Kept tally: attribution rules as a closed list of simulation events that add a person, and the events that remove them.
   - Arrival ceremonies, the promises ledger, hope inheritance in the second generation, and the honest ceiling.
7. **Satire: proclamations.**
   - Channel spec: sender, delivery through the dispatch drawer, frequency caps, and the rule that each proclamation is tied to one real event.
   - The throughline that every archon is fundraising for the Joining.
   - A style guide per archon for Heliarch, Anodyne, and Strategos's court: vocabulary drawn from the corporate liturgy, what the archon fails to notice, and **three example proclamations each**, marked PROPOSED.
8. **Fun.** Execution replay (knowledge-honest; survivor testimony fills in), plan-comes-together stings, praise of leader improvisation.
9. **Affect map by movement.** Expand Execution Plan §2.7 into one row per Movement plus the twenty-year cut: the intended awe, despair, and hope beats; the simulated event that causes each; and the Phase that builds it.
10. **Acceptance for later phases.** For each feeling gate in Execution Plan §5, a concrete observation protocol: what to watch for, and what question to ask the tester afterwards.

Style: specification, not essay. Tables where content is tabular. Cite every existing-canon dependency. Mark every invention PROPOSED.

## Acceptance
- All ten sections present; every item in Execution Plan §2 is covered (list the mapping in the report).
- Every affect formula uses only integer arithmetic and named inputs.
- Nine example proclamations total, each tied to a named simulated event, each using at least one term from the corporate liturgy table.
- Citations resolve (grep evidence in report).
- No new archon powers: every archon behaviour cited to the World Bible.

## Out of scope
Implementation. Music composition. Archons other than Heliarch, Anodyne, Autophagos, and Strategos, except in the affect map.

## Review focus (Liam)
The Roll, the Kept tally rules, and the nine proclamations. These carry the tone of the whole game.
