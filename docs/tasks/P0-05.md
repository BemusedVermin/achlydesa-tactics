## Specification

### Source research (ADR-0100)
Compare at least two openly available global DEMs, for example SRTM 1-arc-second and Copernicus GLO-30, with its 90 m sibling for surveying. For each, record:
- resolution;
- file format and tiling;
- void handling;
- download method, preferring sources that need no account and giving exact URL patterns;
- the license and attribution requirement, **quoted from the provider's own page, with URL and retrieval date**.

Recommend one. The ADR must also state that raw files live in `data/raw/terrain/` and are never committed, and give the exact commands to download the tiles for each candidate window.

### Candidate windows
Propose 4–6 windows of 150 × 150 km in arid regions with a Levant-like or high-desert character. Survey with 90 m data to keep downloads small. Each window must contain:
1. a long, mostly passable corridor of at least 100 km for the Dry Meridian road;
2. an escarpment or ridge line near that corridor (checkpoint and ambush geography);
3. a secondary watercourse or wadi the road crosses (Reedbank);
4. a zone where one watercourse or pass system offers three separable crossings within about 20 km (Three Crossings);
5. contrasting high ground: plateau, massif, or crater.

The game never uses real place names. Windows may be rotated by multiples of 90° or mirrored later.

### Hillshade renders (`tools/scripts/hillshade.py`)
- Inputs: tile paths, a window (SW lat/lon, size in km).
- Local equirectangular projection. Standard hillshade (azimuth 315°, altitude 45°) blended with elevation tints in the Field Atlas day palette: Low ground `#DDD2B8`, Middle `#BBBBA4`, High `#9C979B` (Field Atlas §3.1).
- Draw a 10 km grid, a scale bar, and a north arrow.
- Output 1500 × 1500 px PNG.
- Annotate candidate positions for features 1–4 as labeled marks, so Liam can see the story in the terrain.

### `TERRAIN_CANDIDATES.md`
Per candidate:
- the image;
- the SW corner and extent;
- the real region, named for Liam's reference only;
- how each required feature is met, with window-local km coordinates;
- elevation range;
- a one-line note on its feel (e.g. "long pale graben under a black basalt rim").

Rank the candidates and recommend one.

## Acceptance
- `pip install -r tools/scripts/requirements.txt && python3 tools/scripts/hillshade.py --help` works.
- Every candidate PNG exists and is regenerated by a command given in the markdown.
- The ADR's license statements each have a provider URL and retrieval date.
- No raw DEM data in the diff (`git diff --stat` shows none under `data/`).
