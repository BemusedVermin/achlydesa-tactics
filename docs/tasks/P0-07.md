# P0-07 — Onboarding design

**Agent:** canon-editor · **Size:** M · **Depends on:** P0-03, P0-06 · **Branch slug:** onboarding · **Runs on:** either

## Goal
Achlydesa is dense. The Red Ledger opening must teach it through the fiction, one idea at a time, without a tutorial map, and must deliver the first awe, despair, and hope beats. Specify each onboarding beat precisely enough to build in Phase 8 and to guide which UI must exist in Phases 2–5.

## Specification

### Beats
At least the seven beats in Execution Plan §7, in play order. For each beat, a block with these fields:
- **Trigger:** a world condition, not a script step (e.g. "Kest's extraction team within 1.5 km of the caravan").
- **Where:** site id(s) from `red_ledger_sites.ron`.
- **Newly visible UI:** Field Atlas §8 inventory names.
- **Verbs available:** from High-Level Design §5.5.
- **What the player does**, and the minimum valid action.
- **What is taught**, in one sentence.
- **Who speaks:** a named character, with the channel (report, request, testimony).
- **Outcomes:** at least two, none requiring reload.
- **Feeling:** awe, despair, or hope, with a reference to the Experience Bible section.
- **Built in:** the Phase that makes it possible.

### Disclosure schedule
A table: UI element or verb → the beat that first shows it → the Field Atlas section. Nothing appears before it is needed. Advanced order-composer fields stay closed until the player first needs a constraint.

### Invariants
- By Reedbank, the player has used every core verb (Move, Hold, Observe, Retreat, Resupply, Evacuate) at least once without opening help.
- No beat pauses the simulation for instruction. All teaching rides legitimate decision points (Field Atlas §19).
- No beat forces an outcome by removing player options. Beats may constrain options only for physical reasons in the simulation.

### Playtest observation sheet
For each beat, the one thing to watch for, and the failure sign that means redesign.

## Acceptance
- Every beat block has all ten fields.
- Every UI name and verb exists in the cited documents (grep evidence).
- The disclosure schedule covers every Field Atlas §8 element used in the beats.

## Out of scope
Dialogue writing beyond one example line per speaking character, marked PROPOSED.
